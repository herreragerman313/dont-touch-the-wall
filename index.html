Don't Touch the Wall

Trace the blue path from the green dot to the gold goal. Touch the black and you start over.

How to play

  • Click the green dot to start a level.
  • Move your mouse along the blue path to the gold goal.
  • Touching the black area, leaving the board, or getting hit by a red dot sends you back to the start of the level.
  • Every level the path gets thinner. Red dots show up on level 4.
  • Levels 7 and 8 are "Lights Out": you can see the map before you click start, then the lights go off and you only see around your cursor.
